Hospitals manage large volumes of information across multiple operational areas, including patient records, doctor details, appointments, medical services, bed occupancy, and financial transactions. When this information is scattered across different systems, departments, or individual records, hospital staff may find it difficult to access relevant information quickly and maintain a unified understanding of hospital operations. Hospital administrators and staff need a centralized solution that brings these different information areas together, allowing them to monitor essential activities, access relevant records, and gain meaningful insights without navigating multiple disconnected sources.

This project aims to:

  • Provide a consolidated overview of hospital operations.
  • Simplify access to patient and doctor information.
  • Monitor appointments, medical activities, and bed occupancy.
  • Track patient charges, hospital revenue, and doctor commissions.
  • Present meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and reports.
  • Support hospital staff and administrators in monitoring activities and making informed decisions.

Hospital Analytics Dashboard

The hospital analytics dashboard brings together fragmented and inconsistent hospital data into a single, centralized reporting interface. It provides key stakeholders with an interactive view of hospital operations, making it easier to access, monitor and understand important information across different departments.

Built using Power BI, the dashboard transforms raw hospital data into meaningful visual reports, enabling stakeholders to explore information through interactive visualizations, filters and KPIs.

Dashboard Pages

Explore the different pages of the Hospital Analytics Dashboard.

01

Overview

The Overview page provides a consolidated summary of important hospital metrics, giving stakeholders a quick understanding of overall hospital operations. It brings together information related to patient discharges, medicine consumption, bed availability, hospital revenue and upcoming appointments.

Discharge Information

Summary of patient discharge information.

Medicine Consumption

Monthly trends in medicine consumption.

Beds Availability

Overview of available and occupied beds.

Revenue Analysis

Revenue earned by the hospital from different services.

Upcoming Appointments

Summary of scheduled upcoming patient appointments.

02

Patient Information

The Patient Information page provides a detailed view of patients currently available in the hospital database. It brings together patient details, medicine consumption and charges associated with the services availed by patients, offering a consolidated view of patient-related information.

Patient Details

Information about patients currently available in the hospital database.

Medicine Consumption

Details of medicines consumed by individual patients.

Service Charges

Charges incurred by patients for the hospital services they have availed.

Medicine Quantity

Quantity of medicines sold or dispensed to patients.

03

Doctor Information

The Doctor Information page provides a consolidated view of individual doctors, including their personal details, upcoming appointments, commission calculations and key performance indicators. It enables users to explore doctor-specific information and calculate estimated commissions on the go.

Doctor Details

Access individual doctor information, including personal details, specialization and department.

Upcoming Appointments

View upcoming appointments scheduled for the selected doctor.

Commission Calculator

Calculate estimated doctor commissions based on patient payments and applicable commission rates.

Doctor KPIs

Monitor key performance indicators associated with the selected doctor.

04

Hospital

The Hospital page provides an overview of hospital operations by bringing together information related to patient tests, doctor appointments, surgeries and bed availability. It enables stakeholders to monitor important medical activities and access operational information in one place.

Patient Age Analysis

View the distribution of patients across different age categories.

Patient Tests

Explore patient test results, completion status and clinical notes.

Doctor Appointments

View doctor-wise appointments along with patient details, reasons and prescribed actions.

Surgery Information

Access surgery schedules, dates, timings and related patient information.

Bed Availability

Monitor available and occupied beds across different room categories.

05

Finance

The Finance page provides a consolidated view of the hospital's financial performance. It brings together billing information, patient charges, salary expenses, doctor commissions, medicine sales and stock details, allowing stakeholders to monitor important financial metrics in one place.

Hospital KPIs

Monitor patient count, doctor count, staff count, total bills and total bill amount.

Doctor & Staff Salaries

Track total and average doctor salaries, along with staff salary expenses.

Doctor Commissions

Monitor doctor commission expenses associated with hospital operations.

Monthly Medicine Sales

Analyse medicine sales trends across different months.

Patient Charges Analysis

Explore charges generated from surgery, room, tests, other services, fees and discounts.

Medicine Stock Status

Compare medicine stock and sales levels, including supplier-wise stock information.
